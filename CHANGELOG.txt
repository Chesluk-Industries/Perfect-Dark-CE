Perfect Dark PC port - ragdoll physics build
Changes since the 2026-10-10 build
==================================

  See PLAYER_GUIDE.txt for every setting, its default and the controls.

RAGDOLLS AND BODIES
  - Bodies no longer slide down every slope like a slide: they hold on
    ordinary ramps and only slide off steep ones (pd.ini: RagdollSlopeGrip).
  - Knocked-out guards (tranquilizer, punches, all knockouts) become
    ragdolls. They stay alive, unless they fall from a great height.
  - Guards who see a body come over, kneel by it and search the area, and
    they also check on knocked-out bodies.
  - Held Bodies Stop Bullets (Ragdoll Interactions): a body you hold
    between you and a guard takes his shots.
  - Doors: a body caught in a closing door - an arm or a leg too - stops it,
    and the door opens again after a moment.
  - Glass is tougher: bodies only break normal glass when thrown hard, and
    bulletproof glass only breaks from explosions.
  - Blood sprays from wounds whenever there's dismemberment.
  - Shielded characters (Cassandra in Extraction) take a thrown body on
    their shield instead of stumbling, so their scripted walk carries on.
  - Bodies in the air for 1.5 seconds (flung by a blast, a throw or a punch,
    or falling) scream, as guards falling over a railing do, and the cry
    follows the body: bodies that haven't come to rest yet (or are sent
    flying within 2 seconds of first coming to rest), and knocked-out
    guards (pd.ini: RagdollScreamAirTime). The air time only counts once
    the body is free of its death animation. Now and then (1
    in 100) a man lets out a rarer scream instead (data/sounds/scream.wav),
    placed and following the body like the others.
  - Grabbing (hold Use) works in the Ext control style with either Use
    button (A on modern controllers didn't grab).
  - The Laser's short-range stream can dismember when Damage Needed is at
    its minimum (0.5).
  - Cut-off pieces (head, arms, legs, torso) you carry now hit their own
    body instead of passing through it, doors and objects where a piece is
    (not just where the rest of the body is) stop it, and a piece thrown at
    someone far from the rest of the body hits them and staggers them.
  - Pieces carried far from the rest of the body no longer vanish.
  - A body you're holding thuds when you swing it into a wall, a door or
    another body (dragging it over the floor stays quiet), and cut pieces
    far from the rest of their body are heard where they are.
  - Grab Strength now defaults to 1.20x.
  - pd.ini: the ragdoll settings have their own [Ragdoll] section (they were
    in [Game]). An older pd.ini is still read, and its ragdoll settings are
    moved there the next time it's saved.

DEATH CAMERA
  - While dead, the move keys make your body twitch and looking turns its
    head (and the camera past what the neck allows), as fast as looking
    while alive: your mouse speed, stick sensitivity and Look Up/Down
    setting apply. In the Ext control style the sticks work as while alive
    (the look stick looks, the move stick twitches).
  - New option: Manual Skip (Death Camera..., under Immersive Death Camera).
    The death screen waits until you press Start or Fire.
  - Guards aim at your body's head after you die.

NEW CHEAT: DEADLY PUNCH
  - Unlocked like Hurricane Fists (Extraction, Agent, under 2:03). Punches
    kill instead of knocking out, throw bodies hard and can split a body at
    the waist. Hurricane Fists is back to the original.

HIGH FRAMERATES (above 60 FPS)
  - Guards on patrol no longer freeze, and guards moving off screen no longer
    crawl at a quarter of their speed.
  - Guards no longer spot you, leave cover or roll their dice faster than at
    60 FPS; pistol guards no longer fire extra bullets in bursts.
  - Levels end properly (the outro started but never finished).
  - WAR!: Maians and Skedar no longer shoot their own side at the start.
  - Extraction (and The Duel): the walk into the bodyguards' room no longer
    breaks and leaves you stuck facing a wall.
  - Explosions do their full lasting damage again.
  - Rocket and grenade smoke trails, homing rockets, lightning, sun glare,
    the combat boost effect, the HUD message sound, strafing, leaning,
    guards on stairs, the CamSpy and the teleport effect now behave as at 60
    FPS.
  - The heartbeat and the Laser's stream sound no longer play too fast, and
    the stream no longer doubles up when you strafe.

FIXES
  - Attack Ship: the Skedar sent out during the bridge defence appear
    beside the Skedar sending them, not inside him (the two could end up
    stuck in each other, more often at high framerates).
  - Deep Sea: in the intro, the lift's motor sound stops as soon as the lift
    is at rest at the bottom, and when the lift heads back up after the
    cutscene its moving sound plays straight away (not only from the next
    stop).

ACCURATE PLAYER COLLISION
  - Now experimental and off by default: Player Collision moved to
    Extended > Physics > Debug as "Player Collision (Experimental)", on
    Vanilla (the game's own collision). An existing pd.ini keeps its
    setting. Bodies still use the accurate collision.
  - Scripted walks (Extraction, The Duel) use the game's own collision.
  - Area 51 (hangar and control room lifts): you can no longer walk out
    through the sides of the lift, and bodies stay inside it too.

LIGHTS (by joshuarwood)
  - Our own lens flare and light glare changes are replaced by joshuarwood's
    light work for the PC port (his pull requests #714, #722, #735 and #760
    on perfect-dark-pc-port/perfect_dark):
    - The gun and hands hide light glares behind them, tested against the
      gun's model at the depth the N64 used (#714).
    - Lights that were missing on PC are back in Defection, Villa and
      Investigation, and the Dr. Caroll cutscene no longer loses its light:
      the level files are patched at start-up into an "autopatch" folder in
      the game's home folder (not with --moddir; --no-patches skips them).
      Jon's double line in the Infiltration outro is fixed there too (#722).
    - Light glares show through grated doors (#735).
    - Split screen (co-op, counter-op and multiplayer) gets light glares,
      suns and lens flares, each view its own (Options > Extended > Video >
      Splitscreen Light Glares, on by default) (#760).
    - Fixed a crash when the credits (or any screen without a level)
      start, in the split-screen glare code.

AUDIO
  - 5.1 speaker levels (Extended > Audio > Levels...), greyed out on stereo
    devices; Options > Audio > Surround Setup opens them while Sound is on
    Surround.
  - Dolby Surround: in stereo with Sound on Surround, the sound is Dolby
    Surround encoded as on the N64.
  - Sounds moving between speakers pan smoothly instead of stuttering.

SOURCE
  - The code is split into the port fixes and the ragdoll module, and the
    fixes build on their own (no change in behaviour).
  - The source folder of a release says what each change is: a fix (the
    port behaving as the original), a non-vanilla change, or ragdoll /
    physics (SOURCE_GUIDE.txt, CHANGED_FILES.txt, MOD_INDEX.txt), and
    includes the two patches of the split.


Changes in the 2026-10-10 build (since the 2026-10-04_2214 build)
==================================================================

REMOVED FROM THIS BUILD
  - Settings that no longer exist:
      Ragdoll Collision: ragdolls always use the accurate (drawn) collision,
        except in the Combat Simulator.
      Blend Time, Blend Hold: replaced by the new animation blend (below).

RAGDOLLS
  - Animation blend reworked. "Animation Blend" is now a chance (% of
    deaths, default 80%). The death animation plays out with the body
    following it, and physics takes over when the animation ends, on a
    headshot, when the body touches a wall, object, character or another
    body, or when there's no floor under it (ledges). The animation follows
    slopes, stairs and moving lifts, and a shot makes the hit limb jolt.
  - Ranged settings: Stiffness Level, Stiffness Fade Time and Reflex
    Strength now have a Min and a Max. Each body picks its own value in
    between, so deaths vary.
  - Preset (Extended > Physics > Preset): "Default" puts every ragdoll and
    dismemberment setting back to its default; any change shows "Custom".
  - New setting: Headshot: Lifeless Drop (part of headshot deaths take no
    push from the shot and simply drop).
  - New setting: Reflex: Catch on Ledge. An arm or leg left on a ledge
    holds the body for a moment as it goes over.
  - New setting: Guards React to Bodies. A body thrown into a guard
    staggers him, and a body moved into a guard's view counts as a death he
    has seen. Guards no longer see bodies behind their backs.
  - Characters Push Bodies:
      Off: walking over a body no longer moves it. Everything else still
        collides (bodies falling, sliding or thrown into someone).
      On: walking into bodies nudges them gently.
  - The world reacts to bodies:
      impact sounds, dust and splashes that match the floor surface;
      bodies slide differently on wood, stone, carpet, metal, mud, snow...;
      bodies break glass and push light objects;
      swinging doors push bodies, and a closing door stops at a body
      instead of passing through it.
  - Scripted deaths (the Villa snipers' falls, the Chicago guard going over
    the railing) turn into ragdolls when the animation ends.
  - Office chairs: the backrest is thicker for bodies, so they don't fall
    through it.
  - Arms no longer sink into the chest, and a body already on the floor no
    longer pulls a hand into its chest.
  - Reaper: only the spinning blades cut limbs, and grinding a body cuts it.
  - Co-op: both players can grab bodies and objects; your old body is
    cleared when you respawn; bodies no longer vanish when there are many.
  - New defaults (all changeable, and restored by the Default preset):
      Push Strength 0.8 (was 1.0), Animation Blend 80% (was off),
      Stiffness Level 1.0-3.0 (was 1.0), Stiffness Fade Time 1.0-3.8 s
      (was 3.4 s), Reflex Strength 1.2-2.0 (was 1.0), Brace Fall Chance 53%
      (was 20%), Clutch Wound Chance 53% (was 70%), Headshot: No Reaction
      40% (was 50%), Headshot: Lifeless Drop 40%, Dismemberment Chance 40%
      (was 80%), H Key Spawns Dummy off.

ACCURATE PLAYER COLLISION
  - Ladders work exactly as in the original game.
  - Invisible floors the game has (such as the shaft separators in
    Defection) are kept, so you and bodies land on them as in the original.
  - Railings, grates and glass panes are thicker, so bodies and the player
    don't slip through.

HIGH FRAMERATES (above 60 FPS)
  - Smooth gun animations (no magazine shake), recoil and muzzle flash.
  - Fixed at high FPS: drone and chopper guns, autoguns, footsteps, sparks,
    slow motion at 240 FPS, decloaking, the Infiltration cutscene flash,
    doors, lifts, crouching, turrets, hover vehicles, explosion shake,
    weather, Dr Caroll's faces, the sneeze sound, sparking broken lights,
    and many other timers that ran too fast.
  - Somersault and roll animations no longer twitch mid-roll.
  - Extraction: the keycard guard now always appears (at 240 FPS Dr Caroll
    could count as "under attack" before the level started).

GAMEPLAY
  - The Falcon 2's sideways pose no longer stops it from firing.

GRAPHICS
  - Co-op and Counter-op now get the same effects as solo: suns and lens
    flares, light glare, stars, flying cars, bullet impacts, smoke, glass
    shards and the full decal budget.
  - Split screen: light glare and lens flares no longer flicker, and the
    gun now hides flares behind it in every view.
  - Crossbow: stripes at the edge of its textures fixed.
  - Shiny surfaces no longer turn see-through in the dark (fix ported from
    murkantor's perfect_dark_netplay).
  - New defaults: GUI Texture Filtering off, Unlimited Decals on, Clamp
    Decals to Edges on.

AUDIO
  - 5.1 surround output (Extended > Audio), with a speaker test. With the
    game's Sound mode set to Surround, sounds behind you come from the rear
    speakers.
  - Four times the sound capacity: sounds no longer cut out in busy fights.

STABILITY
  - Fixed a crash when loading a mission after changing key bindings.
  - The ragdoll code was reorganised and optimised (no change in behaviour).
