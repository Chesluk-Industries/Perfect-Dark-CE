1. SETTING UP
-------------
  1. Put your own Perfect Dark ROM in the data folder, named
     pd.ntsc-final.z64 (NTSC 1.1 / "final"). The ROM isn't included.
  2. Run pd.x86_64.exe. To report a problem, use run_with_log.bat instead:
     it also writes pd.log next to the exe.

  Settings are saved in pd.ini. To go back to this build's defaults, delete
  pd.ini (or use Options > Extended > Physics > Preset > Default for the
  physics settings).


2. WHAT THIS BUILD ADDS
-----------------------
  Ragdoll physics
    Bodies fall, tumble and slide for real. Shots, blasts and characters push
    them around, they can be grabbed and dragged, and they react to what kills
    them.

  Accurate collision
    Bodies collide with the level as it's drawn, not only with the game's
    simplified collision, so they no longer fall through gaps or slip
    through railings the original missed. The same for you is an
    experimental option (Physics > Debug > Player Collision).

  Fixes and improvements to the port
    - Plays correctly above 60 FPS: guards, scripts, weapons, effects, sounds
      and timers all run at the right speed at any frame rate.
    - 5.1 surround output, Dolby Surround in stereo, smooth sound panning.
    - Bullet holes, blood and scorch marks: no limit on how many stay, and
      they no longer hang off the edges of surfaces.
    - Shiny surfaces (floors, glass) look right in the dark again.
    - Light fixes by joshuarwood: the gun hides light glares behind it,
      missing lights restored in Defection, Villa and Investigation, glares
      through grated doors, and glares in split screen.
    - Texture glitches on some rock and wall textures are fixed.
    - Co-op gets the full single player effects (smoke, sparks, corpses).
    - A crash writes a log you can send in (see section 12).


3. RAGDOLLS AND BODIES
----------------------
  Dying
    - Most deaths start with the game's death animation and hand over to
      physics as the body hits something, loses its footing or gets shot
      again. Headshots can drop a guard instantly.
    - Dying guards react: they stumble when shot running, brace their fall,
      clutch the wound, catch themselves on ledges and protect their head.
    - Strong weapons, explosions and a full shotgun blast to one limb can take
      limbs off (and, rarely, split the body at the waist). Blood sprays from
      the wounds, and blood splats carry round corners. With Paintball Mode
      on, the cut ends are filled with paint colours instead of red and
      spray paint.
    - Knocked-out guards (tranquilizer, punches) also become ragdolls. They
      stay alive, unless they fall from a great height.
    - Ride the hoverbike into an enemy with a run-up at close to its top
      speed and you run him over: he's killed and knocked down a few steps
      ahead of the bike, and the bike carries on. A slower hit, or revving
      against him, only bumps him as before. Friendly characters are never
      run over.

  Bodies in the world
    - Bodies slide down steep slopes but stay put on ordinary ramps, float in
      water, and are pushed by explosions, gunfire and moving doors and lifts.
    - A body (even an arm or leg) caught in a closing door stops it, and the
      door opens again after a moment.
    - Bodies thrown hard enough break normal glass. Bulletproof glass still
      only breaks from explosions.
    - Corpses stay around much longer than in the original game.

  Guards and bodies
    - Guards who see a body (or one being moved or thrown) come to look at
      it, kneel by it and then search the area.
    - A body thrown at a guard staggers him and gives you away. Shielded
      characters (like Cassandra) only take it on their shield.
    - Guards only notice a death they could actually see happen: a guard
      looking the other way doesn't.

  Grabbing
    - Hold Use while looking at a body or a loose object nearby to pick it
      up; let go to drop it. In the Ext control style either Use button
      works (A or B on a modern controller). A body held between you and a guard takes the
      guard's shots for you.


4. CONTROLS
-----------
  Hold Use ............. grab a body or object (Grab must be on)
  Move keys ............ while dead: your body twitches
  Look (mouse/stick) ... while dead: look around through your body's eyes
  Start or Fire ........ while dead with Manual Skip on: continue
  H .................... spawn a test dummy (only with H Key Spawns Dummy on)

  Nothing else changes: all the game's own controls work as before.


5. THE DEATH CAMERA
-------------------
  With Immersive Death Camera on, when you die you see the fall through your
  own body's eyes, and your gun drops from your hands.
    - The move keys make the body twitch; looking turns the head and, past
      what the neck allows, the camera. Looking turns as fast as it does
      while alive: your mouse speed, stick sensitivity and Look Up/Down
      setting apply.
    - Guards keep firing at your body's head for a moment.
    - With Manual Skip on, the death screen waits until you press Start or
      Fire, so you can watch what happens to the body.


6. OPTIONS > EXTENDED > PHYSICS
-------------------------------
  Defaults are in brackets. The same menu opens from the Combat Simulator:
  Options, then More Options > Physics.

  Preset [Default]
    Default puts every physics setting back to this build's defaults
    (gravity included). It shows Custom once you change something.
  Ragdolls [On]
    Off plays exactly like the original game (animated deaths).

  Ragdoll Behaviours...
    Animation Blend [80%]
      Share of deaths that start with the death animation before physics
      takes over (the rest go limp at once).
    Body Stiffness [On]
      Dying bodies keep some muscle tension for a few seconds.
    Stiffness Level Min / Max [1.00 / 3.00]
    Stiffness Fade Time Min / Max [1.0 s / 3.8 s]
      How stiff the body starts, and how long it takes to go limp. Each death
      picks a value between Min and Max.
    Reactions...
      Reflex: Stumble [On] ........ shot while running, the legs carry on
      Reflex: Head Guard [On] ..... the neck keeps the head from slamming down
      Reflex: Catch on Ledge [On] . a dying body can grab a ledge edge
      Reflex Strength Min / Max ... [1.20 / 2.00] how strong the reflexes are
      Brace Fall Chance [53%] ..... the arms reach out to break the fall
      Clutch Wound Chance [53%] ... a hand goes to the wound
      Headshot: No Reaction [40%] . headshots that go limp at once
      Headshot: Lifeless Drop [40%] of those, ones that just drop (no push)
    Dismemberment...
      Dismemberment [On]
      Explosions Dismember [On]
      Torso Can Split [On]
      Dismemberment Chance [40%] .. when the hit is strong enough
      Damage Needed [2.0] ......... weapon damage needed (Falcon 2 = 1,
                                    DY357 Magnum = 2); at 0.5 the
                                    Laser's stream cuts too
    Death Camera...
      Immersive Death Camera [On]
      Manual Skip [Off]
      Smoothing [50%] ............. smooths the camera's motion
      Body Awareness [80%] ........ how much the camera keeps your body in view

  Ragdoll Interactions...
    Characters Push Bodies [Off]
      On: walking into bodies shoves them. Off: you and guards step over them
      (a body thrown at someone still hits them).
    Grab Bodies and Objects (Hold Use) [On]
    Held Bodies Stop Bullets [On]
    Guards React to Bodies [On]
    Push Strength [0.80x]
      How hard shots, blasts and characters push bodies.
    Grab Strength [1.20x]

  Gravity...
    Gravity [100%]
      -500% to 500% of the original gravity (1% steps from -100% to
      100%, 5% steps beyond) for the ragdolls, items (dropped guns,
      things knocked loose or blown off) and bullet casings, sparks and
      glass shards. Below 100% falling bodies don't scream. At 0% they
      drift until they hit something. Below 0% gravity is inverted:
      bodies and items fall up to the ceiling, and under open sky they
      leave the level and are removed (as when something falls out of
      it). Bodies lying still wake up when you change it. Items stop at
      the same ceilings bodies do (at any gravity, 100% included) and,
      when it isn't 100%, bounce off the same walls. In low gravity
      casings still in the air long after they'd normally have landed
      land without the clink.
    Player Gravity [100%]
      5% to 500% (1% steps up to 100%, 5% steps beyond): how fast you,
      guards and simulants fall. Falling for 4 seconds still kills you;
      at low gravity it takes longer.
    What players, simulants and guards throw or fire (grenades, mines,
    knives, rockets) keeps the original gravity. The Preset's Default puts
    both back to 100%.

  Performance...
    Singleplayer / Co-Op
      Max Bodies [Unlimited]
        How many corpses stay as ragdolls; past this the oldest fades out.
        Vanilla: bodies fade away as in the original (off screen, or when
        too many are in view; about 10 in good conditions). 12 to 256, or
        Unlimited.
      Max Active Bodies [Unlimited]
        How many of them keep moving physically; older ones freeze where
        they lie. 12 to 256, or Unlimited. Lower these on a slow PC.
    Combat Simulator
      Max Bodies [32]
        Vanilla: a body disappears when its player or simulant respawns, as
        in the original. 12 to 256, or Unlimited: the body stays where it
        fell, and past this many the oldest fades out.
      Max Active Bodies [32]
        As above, for the Combat Simulator.
    Resting Bodies Turn Solid [Combat Simulator]
      Combat Simulator, Both or Off. A body turns solid as soon as it has
      finished its death reactions and lies still (no part of it still
      falling or settling). Other bodies still land on a solid body and
      shots still hit it, but nothing moves it any more, so it costs next
      to no physics time. Doors pass through solid bodies. Only a moving
      lift, or glass breaking under it, makes one move again. A body
      already lying still when its player or simulant respawns turns solid
      straight away. In singleplayer (Both), picking a solid body up or
      cutting a limb off it makes it move again too.

  Debug...
    H Key Spawns Dummy [Off] ... H spawns a test guard in front of you
    Player Collision (Experimental) [Vanilla]
      Vanilla ... the game's own collision only (exactly as the original)
      Accurate .. the level as it's drawn
      Both ...... the game's collision, with the drawn level filling the gaps
      Experimental: Accurate and Both can still let you through or stop you
      in places the original doesn't. Ladders, the Combat Simulator and the
      scripted walks (Extraction, The Duel) always use the game's own
      collision.
    Collision View [Off] ........ shows the collision around you


7. VIDEO AND AUDIO OPTIONS
--------------------------
  Options > Extended > Video
    Unlimited Decals [On]
      Bullet holes, blood and scorch marks stay instead of the oldest
      disappearing. Takes effect on the next level.
    Clamp Decals to Edges [On]
      Decals no longer hang off the edge of the surface they're on.
    Splitscreen Light Glares [On]
      Light glares, suns and lens flares in split screen (co-op,
      counter-op and multiplayer), each view its own. By joshuarwood.

  Options > Extended > Audio
    5.1 Surround Output [On]
      With the game's Sound option set to Surround and a 5.1 (or larger)
      speaker setup, the game outputs real 5.1. The line below it shows what
      is being output.
    Levels...
      A volume for each speaker (Front Left, Front Right, Centre, Subwoofer,
      Rear Left, Rear Right), 0 to 150%.
    Speaker Test
      Plays a sound from each speaker in turn.
    These are greyed out on a stereo device.

  Dolby Surround
    With stereo speakers or headphones and the game's Sound option on
    Surround, the sound is Dolby Surround encoded as on the N64, for a Dolby
    Pro Logic receiver. There's no setting: it follows the Sound option.
  Options > Audio > Surround Setup
    A shortcut to the 5.1 settings, shown while Sound is set to Surround.

  Options > Extended > Game > Name Banner... (for each player)
    Show Name Banner [Off]
      Your name in a box at the bottom left of the screen, like a pickup
      message, with a light running round its border. It comes in once
      the mission starts and goes for cutscenes. In the Combat Simulator
      it's your profile name; in solo and co-op, the Banner Name below.
      Based on the banner in murkantor's netplay fork.
    Colour [Green]
      Green, Teal/Purple, Red, Blue, Gold or Custom (pd.ini).
    Banner Name
      The name on your banner in solo and co-op (up to 15 characters).
      Until one is set, player 1's banner shows the agent file's name and
      the others show "Player 2", "Player 3" and "Player 4".

  Options > Extended > Game > Body Awareness [Off] (for each player)
    Your own body in first person: look down to see your legs and torso,
    animated as you move. It only shows once you look down past 30
    degrees, rising into view as you look further down. The head and arms
    are left out (the first person gun and hands are drawn as usual).
    Works in solo, co-op and the Combat Simulator.


8. THE DEADLY PUNCH CHEAT
-------------------------
  A new cheat, unlocked like Hurricane Fists (Extraction on Agent in under
  2:03), and switched on in the Cheats menu like any other. Your punches
  kill instead of dizzying or knocking guards out (from behind too), throw
  bodies hard and occasionally split a body at the waist. The gun-disarm move
  stays a knockout.


9. SETTINGS ONLY IN PD.INI
--------------------------
  A few settings have no menu entry. Change them in pd.ini, in [Game],
  [Ragdoll] or [Video], while the game isn't running. (All the ragdoll
  settings are in [Ragdoll]; a pd.ini from an earlier build that has them
  under [Game] is still read, and they're moved the next time it's saved.)

  [Game]
    WitnessFieldOfView=1
      1: guards only notice a death within their field of view.
      0: anywhere they have a line of sight (the original).
    Player1.NameBannerText=
      The Banner Name (Player2..Player4 too). Empty it here to go back to
      the default name: the menu's keyboard won't accept an empty name.
    Player1.NameBannerRGB=00FF00
    Player1.NameBannerHighlightRGB=80FF80
      The banner's colours with its Colour on Custom, in hex
      (RRGGBB): the box and name, and the light running round it.
    BodyAwarenessBack=32
      How far behind the view your body is drawn with Body Awareness on
      (0 to 80). Higher keeps the chest out of the view when looking down.
      When crouching, the upper body is pushed back further, so the view
      never ends up inside it.
    BodyAwarenessPitch=30
      How far down (degrees, 0 to 80) you must look before your body shows.
      0 shows it all the time.
    BodyAwarenessDrop=0
      Draws your body this much lower (0 to 60).

  [Ragdoll]
    RagdollDeathCamControl=1
      0 turns off the twitching and looking while dead.
    RagdollSlopeGrip=0.70
      How well bodies hold on slopes (0 to 1.5). 0.70 holds them on slopes
      up to about 35 degrees; higher holds them on steeper ones. 0 lets them
      slide on almost any slope.
    RagdollGlassBreakSpeed=420
      How fast a body must hit normal glass to break it.
    RagdollDeadlyPunchPushScale=3.0
      How hard Deadly Punch throws bodies.
    RagdollScreamAirTime=1.5
      Seconds a body (a fresh death, one sent flying within 2 s of first
      coming to rest, or a knocked-out guard) must be in the air, flying or
      falling, before it screams, counted once it's free of its death
      animation. 0: never. 1 in 100 of the
      men's screams is data/sounds/scream.wav (keep it with the game;
      without it they use the game's cries only).
    ClipMaxCatchFall=600
      With accurate player collision: a drawn floor further below than this (from
      where a fall began) doesn't catch you, so pits stay deadly.
    ClipRenderFloors=1, ClipRenderCeilings=1, ClipRenderWalls=1
      Which kinds of drawn surfaces accurate collision uses.
    TickRateDivisor
      The port's own setting. 0 runs the game logic at your frame rate (see
      section 10).

  [Video]
    ShinyAlphaFloor=255
      How solid shiny room surfaces stay in the dark (255 = solid, 0 = the
      original fade).


10. HIGH FRAME RATES
--------------------
  With the port's TickRateDivisor set to 0 the game runs at your monitor's
  frame rate. This build fixes the problems that caused: guards stuck on
  patrol or moving at the wrong speed, levels that didn't end, scripted
  scenes breaking, weapons firing too fast or too slow, explosions doing
  too little damage, smoke trails, lightning, sounds playing too fast and
  more. Everything should behave as it does at 60 FPS.


11. CO-OP AND MULTIPLAYER
-------------------------
  - Co-op and Counter-Operative get ragdolls, both death cameras, and the
    full single player effects.
  - The Combat Simulator has ragdolls too, for players and simulants alike,
    with the Immersive Death Camera and dismemberment. Bodies can't be
    grabbed there. Bodies stay after a respawn, up to the Combat Simulator
    Max Bodies (Physics > Performance; Vanilla: they go with their owner).
  - The Combat Simulator uses the game's own collision for players.


12. PROBLEMS AND CRASH LOGS
---------------------------
  - If the game crashes, it writes pd.crash-<date>-<time>.log (and
    pd.crash.log) next to the exe. Please send it, together with pd.log from
    run_with_log.bat. If it freezes instead, the same log is written after
    10 seconds (Windows).
  - If the game runs slowly with lots of bodies, lower Max Active Bodies.
  - To play as close to the original as possible: Ragdolls off (Player
    Collision is on Vanilla by default) and WitnessFieldOfView=0 in pd.ini.
    Blood still wraps round corners.
